# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about ideas, not people.

## The standard

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. What that means in practice:

**Expected:** Showing empathy and kindness. Being respectful of differing
opinions and experience. Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them. Focusing on what is
best for the project and the people using it.

**Unacceptable:** Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention of any
kind. Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks. Public or private
harassment. Publishing others' private information without permission. Anything
else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a confidential issue or contacting the maintainers
directly. Reports will be reviewed promptly and in confidence, and the reporter's
privacy will be respected.

Maintainers who do not act in good faith on this may face consequences
determined by the project's leadership.
